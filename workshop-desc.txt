The naval design of UTTNH&RT56 follows the vanilla naval designer, and the AI cannot design ships, which greatly reduces the playability of naval battles in the middle and late stages of the game. VNR solves these two problems, but the naval technology tree of VNR only reaches 1955, and the three are incompatible with each other.

By using this mod, VNR can be made compatible with UTTNH&RT56. At the same time, VNR's naval designer and AI naval design are used, and the technology is guided to the 1990s.
